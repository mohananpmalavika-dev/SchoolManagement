Operational Crisis

The 5 Crippling Pain Points Kenyan Schools Face Every Term

Manual administration, uncollected fees, and teacher exhaustion prevent schools from thriving.

📉 1. Severe Fee Arrears & Bad Debt

20% to 35% of student term fees remain unpaid at term end. With no automated tracking, schools face acute cashflow crises, delayed teacher payroll, and strained vendor credit.

Average loss: KES 800,000+ per term
⏳ 2. Manual M-Pesa & Slip Matching

Bursars waste 20+ hours a week manually checking M-Pesa transaction SMS messages, comparing bank counter slips, and handwriting receipt books. Fake M-Pesa screenshots go undetected.

20+ admin hours wasted every week
📝 3. CBC Assessment Burden

Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) demands detailed formative rubrics and personalized learner remarks. Teachers spend weeks manually writing reports instead of teaching.

High teacher burnout & turnover
💬 4. WhatsApp Chaos & Lost Letters

Scattered WhatsApp groups, lost paper circulars, and unanswered phone calls create friction between parents and school leadership. Fee reminders are ignored or lost in chat clutter.

Parent dissatisfaction & mistrust
👁️ 5. Executive Blindspots & Multi-Campus Disconnect

School Directors and Board members cannot see live school performance. How much cash was collected today in KES? What is today's school-wide attendance rate? Who is at risk of dropping out? Without modern software, decisions are based on guesswork.

Zero real-time data for board decisions
