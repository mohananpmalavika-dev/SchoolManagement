# Government reporting implementation summary

The persisted workflow covers period-based school reports, independent review, real CSV/XLSX/JSON exports, manual submission receipts, portal outcomes, rejected-report replacement, quality audits, checklist evidence, deadline alerts and append-only audit records.

Candidate registrations, official results, examination timetable entries and irregularity references can be recorded manually from official documents. Entries retain their source reference and correction history; they do not represent API synchronization.

See [Government reporting](docs/GOVERNMENT_REPORTING.md) for the maintained scope and verification instructions. Earlier claims of automatic NEMIS/KNEC synchronization, universal official template compatibility and 100% compliance are withdrawn.
