# School leave rules

Configure policies in **HR & Payroll → HR setup → Leave types**. Existing types retain annual upfront grants, calendar-day charging, half days and backdated applications. New policies default to 1 January of the current school-local calendar year; set the effective start explicitly when importing an older policy. An optional effective end limits requests and monthly earning. A new policy does not earn leave for years before its effective start.

Each policy defines annual entitlement, annual-upfront or monthly accrual, joining-year proration, carry-forward cap, waiting days from joining, minimum notice, maximum chargeable days per request, backdating and half-day permissions. Entitlements and caps use half-day increments. Used policies are immutable to protect existing balances and requests. A separately configured leave type has its own entitlement; creating a type does not transfer balances or usage from another type.

Monthly earning credits eligible calendar months on their final calendar date in the school timezone. Only full eligible months count unless joining-year proration is enabled; then the first eligible month contributes its eligible-day fraction. Cumulative earning rounds down to half days, so repeated accrual never compounds rounding. Annual-upfront proration uses eligible calendar days in the first eligible year, including leap years. The waiting period and policy start both constrain eligibility. Monthly earning stops at the approved exit date or policy end; future-year entitlement cannot be borrowed early.

Carry-forward uses prior unused entitlement up to the configured cap, including prior years without a materialized balance when the policy was effective then. Recorded earlier legacy grants are preserved. Prior-year pending leave must be resolved before carrying its balance forward. Expired policies and employment that ended before the new year do not carry usable balances into that year.

Staff see live balances before their first request. **GET leaves/balances** calculates entitlement, carry-forward, used, pending and available days without writing records. Applications automatically materialize earned balances and reserve pending days within the same tenant-locked transaction. Managers can also choose **Accrue staff leave balances** for an employee/year. Repeating the calculation for the same date leaves allocations and audit counts unchanged. A recalculation below already committed leave stops for reconciliation rather than silently removing used or reserved days.

Calendar policies count inclusive dates. Working-day policies count only the configured weekdays (1 = Monday through 7 = Sunday). Explicit excluded dates apply to either basis. Requests store their chargeable dates, so payroll and overlap checks do not count an excluded date merely because it lies inside a request range. Split applications at calendar-year boundaries; applications spanning two years are rejected.

Half-day requests require one chargeable date and an AM or PM session. Complementary sessions can coexist, including across paid and unpaid types. The same session, a full day or an ambiguous legacy half-day blocks overlap. Pending and approved requests both reserve their dates. Approval rechecks existing approved leave to catch imported conflicts. Concurrent requests are serialized with balance updates; duplicate sessions cannot reserve twice. Payroll combines both sessions and caps attendance credit to the remaining part of the day. Two approved halves also prevent a personal full-day punch.

Policies can require evidence starting from a configured number of chargeable days. Links must be valid HTTPS URLs without embedded credentials. Supporting evidence starts **PENDING** and must be inspected by another authorized manager through **Review leave document**. The manager records **VERIFIED** or **REJECTED**, findings, authenticated reviewer and review time. Applicants can replace evidence on pending requests; replacement resets verification. A rejected document keeps leave pending and reserved until evidence is replaced, the request is rejected, or it is cancelled. Approval is blocked until submitted or required evidence is verified. Verification is a recorded manual school review; the application does not certify the authenticity of an external document automatically.

Existing historical approvals are preserved by migration. Pending legacy requests with evidence or a document requirement require review. Legacy half days without recorded sessions conservatively block the entire date for overlapping requests. Leave approval, document changes and cancellation respect frozen payroll periods. Only the applicant can replace evidence; another manager must verify documents and approve the applicant's leave. Rejection/cancellation releases pending balance; approved cancellation by another manager releases used balance. All writes and accrual amounts are audited.
