# KRYPTON SCHOOL ERP — PARENT SUPER APP USER MANUAL
**Audience:** Guardians and Parents  
**Access Portal:** `/parent/login` (or PWA on Mobile Devices)  
**Languages:** English & Kiswahili

---

## 1. Accessing Your Parent Account

1. Open the Krypton School Portal link provided by your child's school on your phone or computer.
2. Sign in with your registered mobile phone number or email address.
3. If logging in for the first time, click **First Time Login** to receive a one-time verification SMS code.

---

## 2. Multi-Child Experience

- If you have multiple children enrolled in the school (or across different Krypton-powered schools), all children appear on your unified parent dashboard.
- Tap your child's name or avatar at the top of the screen to switch between:
  - Attendance history and live bus tracking
  - Fee invoices and payment history
  - Competency assessment report cards
  - Homework and teacher messages

---

## 3. Paying School Fees with M-Pesa

1. Select your child and tap **Pay Fees**.
2. Review the outstanding balance and enter the amount you wish to pay.
3. Verify your M-Pesa phone number (format: `07XXXXXXXX` or `01XXXXXXXX`).
4. Tap **Pay via M-Pesa (STK Push)**.
5. An M-Pesa prompt will appear automatically on your phone.
6. Enter your secret M-Pesa PIN and confirm.
7. Within seconds, your payment status updates to **PAID**, and your official PDF receipt is available for instant download.

---

## 4. Submitting Authorized Absence Requests

1. Tap **Absence Request** (`/parent/absence`).
2. Select the dates your child will be absent and the reason (e.g., Medical, Family Emergency).
3. Optionally attach a doctor's note or supporting document.
4. Tap **Submit**. The Class Teacher and Principal receive an immediate notification, and attendance records are automatically marked as `EXCUSED`.
